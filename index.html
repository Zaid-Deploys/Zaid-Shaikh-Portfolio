TRANSPARENCY & CLARITY

Freelance FAQ

Clear, upfront answers about pricing, code ownership, hosting, and post-launch support.

How does your one-time development pricing work?
All projects operate on a fixed, milestone-based pricing model. Following our discovery consultation, you receive a detailed proposal specifying exact deliverables, timelines, and milestones. You only pay for agreed-upon milestones as they are completed. There are zero surprise costs, hidden platform lock-ins, or unexpected hourly overages.
Who owns the website and source code after completion?
You own 100% of everything. Upon final milestone sign-off, full repository access, compiled assets, design source files, and cloud environment credentials are transferred directly to you. You are free to modify, host, or transfer the code without any licensing restrictions or vendor lock-in.
Who is responsible for the domain name and cloud hosting?
You retain direct billing and administrative control over your domain registrar and hosting accounts (e.g. AWS, Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare). I guide you through selecting the optimal, cost-effective provider, configure DNS records, install SSL/TLS certificates, and configure automated deployment pipelines directly into your accounts.
What happens if I need changes or extra features during or after development?
Every milestone includes structured revision rounds to adjust visual styling, copy, and layout. If you decide to introduce major new features outside the initial scope, I provide a transparent, itemized add-on estimate and timeline for your approval before writing a single line of code.
Do you provide maintenance and technical support after launch?
Yes. Every project includes an inclusive 14 to 30-day post-launch warranty covering bug fixes, browser compatibility adjustments, and technical questions at no extra charge. For ongoing needs, I offer flexible monthly retainers for dependency updates, cloud monitoring, performance checks, and continuous feature enhancements.

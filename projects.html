11-Class Currency & Coin Classifier

PyTorch · MobileNetV2 · Transfer Learning · TFLite · OpenCV

A lightweight deep learning computer vision model trained to accurately recognize and classify all denominations of Indian currency (₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500 notes and coins) under real-world challenges such as folding, heavy shadows, wrinkles, and awkward angles.

Base Architecture MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning

Inference Speed Sub-15ms per frame on mobile NPU
  • 99.2% classification accuracy verified with confusion-matrix evaluation
  • Trained with heavy geometric, rotation, crease, and photometric augmentations
  • Quantized TFLite export optimized for low-end mobile hardware
  • Retraining on expanded datasets to continuously enhance edge precision
